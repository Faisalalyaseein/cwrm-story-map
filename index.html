Data sources

  • Basin outline: HydroBASINS v1c, level 6 (HydroSHEDS; Lehner and Grill, 2013). The Euphrates basin is the set of sub-basins that drain to the Euphrates / Tigris junction at Qurna (about 552,000 km²); the Tigris basin is shown for context.
  • Rivers and lakes of the basin, upstream Euphrates: RIMES shapefiles supplied for the project. Publisher, date and licence are to be confirmed.
  • Main Outfall Drain upstream of the project line (Baghdad area to the start of the line): derived from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), about 199 km; the project line itself (529.5 km) is from the Ministry of Water Resources data.
  • Euphrates in Iraq: derived from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Hillah River System, canals, drains, irrigation projects, governorates, monitoring stations and MOD points: CWRM project data (Ministry of Water Resources and GIZ) and the monitoring network design V01 (2026-08-17). Station photographs from the design document.
  • Cities: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Basemap: OpenFreeMap Positron, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors (online; terrain option: OpenTopoMap, CC-BY-SA); without internet the bundled Natural Earth countries map (public domain) is shown.
  • Technology images: illustrative only; source and licence to be confirmed.
  • Statistics: network lengths (2,978 km irrigation, 5,392 km drainage) from the CWRM master GeoPackage (study extent); the 25 km coverage radius is a working assumption, not a design criterion.
